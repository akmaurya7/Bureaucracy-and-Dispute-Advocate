# Project Specification: Bureaucracy & Dispute Advocate

## 1. Objectives
Empower everyday citizens and consumers with automated legal-grade advocacy tools to dispute inflated bills, predatory debt claims, and unlawful corporate fees.

## 2. Team Specialist Roles & Directory Setup
All specialists operate within `D:\projects\Bureaucracy and Dispute Advocate`.

1. **Harvey (Legal Lead)** - Chief Legal Architect & Dispute Strategist
   - Owns statutory accuracy, legal precedent mapping, and demand letter generation logic.
2. **Lex (Backend & OCR)** - Lead Backend & Document Intelligence Engineer
   - Owns document parsing, multimodal vision ingestion, Prisma ORM schema, and API routes.
3. **Mercury (Frontend Lead)** - Lead UI/UX & Full-Stack Frontend Engineer
   - Owns Next.js 15 studio interface, interactive document viewer, and PDF letter export.
4. **Justitia (QA & Compliance)** - Quality Assurance & Statutory Compliance Auditor
   - Owns test suites, citation verifications, statutory deadline validation, and PII protection tests.
